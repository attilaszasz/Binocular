# Epic Details: E016 — Automatic Module Seeding & Additional Official Modules

- **Category**: PRODUCT
- **Priority**: P2
- **Source**: {PRD:CAP-011}{SAD:ADR-0005}

## Scope
Implement automatic discovery and seeding of bundled official modules on app startup. Ship three additional official modules: Panasonic Lumix MFT Cameras, Panasonic Lumix Lenses, and Godox Flashes. All four official modules (including Sony Alpha from E011) are auto-registered and upserted into the database.

- **Key Entities**: Official module files, Seeder service
- **Depends on**: E007 (module engine + validation), E011 (Sony Alpha module pattern)

- **Constraints**: Modules must conform to authoring contract; golden tests; auto-seeded on every startup
